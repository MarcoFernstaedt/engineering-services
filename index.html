How an engagement runs

  1. A short call: what is broken, what it costs you, and whether it is worth fixing at all.
  2. A written scope, a fixed price, a delivery date, and the exclusions spelled out.
  3. Half up front, half on acceptance. You keep the code and a runbook you can read.
  4. If the scope turns out to be wrong, work stops and gets re-scoped in writing — not in an invoice.
